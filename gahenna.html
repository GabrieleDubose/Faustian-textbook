What are Gahennas?

Gahennas are the nickname for when a stigmata goes supercritical and creates a explosion that is compariable to a 50 megaton nuclear bomb going off.

While not containing any radiation, some Gahennas have been recorded to be able to warp reality itself - causing it to stretch, tear, and even crumple like that of a wad of fabric. Most Gaheenas effects will fade after a while, however there are a handful of gaheenas still active today whose effects have yet to fade. Due to their age, researchers have been able to enter the blastfield and record their findings from the inside.

Permament Gaheenas

Babel

The twin to the Wonderland Gahenna, Babel's signiture effect on an area is to warp the architecture to that of impossible euclidian design. Rooms stretch high into the sky, a door that originally led to the next may now lead you to the other side of the world, furniture may defy the laws of gravity itself if it so wishes.

Wonderland

The twin to the Babel Gaheena, Wonderland's primary characteristic is being able to spread and effect the surrounding earth around it. Wonderland itself is made up of the beating flesh of an unknown host, and as such has a tendency to grow into nearby cities and villages. Researchers freqeuntly have to set the flesh on fire to stop it from ruining local infrastructure and causing the populace to develop Jabberwocky Syndrome. Areas that were previously host to this Gaheena (also reffered to as Wonderland trenches) hosts many demons and as such are off limit to the general public due to safety reasons.

Delorian

A paradoxical Gaheena, in technicallity it has not been born yet and yet has been explored well over 10,000 times. This gaheena is born at the end of the world, and as such creates a time loop that reaches into a handful of specified dates. This Gaheena is characterized with its appearance of white synapses firing off and it's ability to create dopplegangers of those who have entered it.
